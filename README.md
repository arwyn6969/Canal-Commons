# 🌊 Canal Commons

### An open, community-driven project to help Britain’s canals thrive.

Canal Commons brings together **canal users, engineers, researchers, communities, businesses, developers, investors and public bodies** to discover practical ways to protect and improve the UK’s inland waterways.

We are deliberately exploring both **public-interest and commercial models**. The objective is simple:

> **Create sustainable value from the canal network and use that value to help keep the network healthy, accessible and useful for future generations.**

This is an experiment. We do not assume we already know the answer.

---

## 🚦 Where we are now

**Phase: Commercial Opportunity Discovery**

We have tested and challenged several early ideas, including canal-monitoring software and tokenisation. Our current evidence suggests that simply building another AI/data platform would not be sufficiently differentiated from capabilities that already exist within the Canal & River Trust and the wider infrastructure sector.

We are therefore investigating whether an independent organisation could add more value by **finding, aggregating, developing and financing canal-related projects that otherwise struggle to happen**.

Current areas of investigation:

- 🚢 freight aggregation
- ⚡ canal energy and utilities
- 🏗 canalside development opportunities
- 💷 project and blended finance
- 🌱 environmental and resilience projects
- 🤝 community-led projects

Nothing is predetermined. A hypothesis is only promoted when evidence supports it.

## 🧭 Our principles

**Evidence before enthusiasm.** We actively try to disprove our own ideas.

**Open by default.** Public research, methods and community knowledge should be reusable wherever licensing permits.

**Commercially sustainable.** The people doing the work need to be paid. We are not assuming that a charitable structure is the only answer.

**Canal benefit matters.** Commercial success is not enough if the resulting activity does not create meaningful public or canal-system value.

**No unnecessary duplication.** We do not want to build something that an existing organisation can already do better unless there is a clear independent reason for us to exist.

**Technology is a tool, not the mission.** AI, GIS, blockchain and tokens are considered only when they solve a real problem.

**Community knowledge counts.** Local and practical knowledge can be as valuable as technical analysis.

---

## 🔓 Open project, commercial discipline

Canal Commons is intended to be an open public project, but **not every future activity will necessarily be public**.

We will keep public wherever possible:

- research and methodology
- public-data analysis
- open tools and code where appropriate
- project concepts
- community contributions
- decisions and lessons learned

We may keep confidential where necessary:

- personal information
- partner confidentiality
- negotiations
- unpublished bids
- commercially sensitive deal information
- proprietary work created for a commercial engagement

If Canal Commons develops a commercial entity, it can operate alongside the open project rather than turning the public repository into a dumping ground for confidential business information.

## 🧪 Our current commercial test

We are looking for a real transaction where Canal Commons can provide a clearly identifiable service and receive legitimate compensation.

The minimum evidence we want is:

**Real project → real counterparty → real problem → quantified economics → defensible Canal Commons role → legitimate revenue → measurable canal/public benefit.**

If we cannot demonstrate that, we will not pretend the business works.

## 📜 Governance and licensing

Canal Commons is currently an open project rather than a finished legal organisation. Governance will evolve as the community and commercial work develop.

We intend to use **appropriate open licences for reusable software, documentation and other material**, while respecting the licences of third-party datasets and protecting legitimate confidential commercial information. See `LICENSE` and `docs/LICENSING-POLICY.md`.

Major changes to mission, governance, commercial structure or community rules should be documented publicly.

## ⚠️ Important

Canal Commons is an independent project. It is **not the Canal & River Trust, the Environment Agency, a government body or any other canal authority**.

Nothing published here should be interpreted as an engineering safety assessment, regulatory approval, investment recommendation or representation on behalf of another organisation.

## 🌊 The long-term ambition

We want to discover whether a modern combination of:

**community + commerce + engineering + technology + capital**

can create a sustainable new model for helping Britain's waterways.

If the evidence says yes, we'll build it.

If the evidence says no, we'll change direction.

**Come help us find out.**
