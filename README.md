# Canal Commons

## Building a commercially sustainable system for healthier UK waterways

Canal Commons is an evidence-first venture exploring how **data, commerce, technology, community participation and capital** can increase the resilience of Britain's inland waterways.

> **Working principle:** make the waterways too useful, too visible and too loved to fail.

## What we are building toward

We are **not** assuming Canal Commons should be a charity, canal operator, freight company, token project or infrastructure owner.

The working hypothesis is a **commercial infrastructure platform** that can:

1. understand canal-system risks and opportunities;
2. turn fragmented information into useful decisions;
3. originate and enable investable projects;
4. reduce costs or create new economic value;
5. earn sustainable commercial revenue;
6. direct an agreed share of value toward waterway resilience.

The final model will be selected by evidence, customer validation and feasibility testing.

## Current hypotheses under test

### H1 — Canal Intelligence
A decision-support layer combining canal infrastructure information with external geospatial, weather, hydrological, planning and other relevant datasets could help prioritise inspection, maintenance, resilience and commercial opportunities.

### H2 — Project Origination
There may be commercially valuable canal-related projects that need independent identification, packaging, counterparties and finance. Canal Commons could earn origination, development, transaction or recurring revenue where it creates genuine additional value.

### H3 — Preventative Maintenance
Additional sensing, remote observation and analytical methods may identify or prioritise risks earlier or more efficiently than conventional approaches. This must be tested against real failure modes and real costs.

### H4 — Value Capture
Canal-related economic activity may be able to contribute a measurable share of value back into waterway resilience. This is a hypothesis, not an entitlement.

## Commercial principles

- **Profit is a requirement, not an afterthought.**
- Customers must have a clear reason to pay.
- Founder and staff compensation must be supportable by commercial revenue.
- Prefer recurring revenue, transaction revenue, licensing, IP and equity/revenue participation over selling hours alone.
- Do not count economic/social value as revenue until an actual capture mechanism exists.
- Do not duplicate capabilities already provided effectively by Canal & River Trust, navigation authorities, IWA or private providers.
- Separate facts, assumptions, calculations and hypotheses.
- Use the cheapest experiment capable of falsifying an important assumption.

## Immediate deliverables

1. Select and document a representative pilot corridor.
2. Build a defensible data inventory and licensing assessment.
3. Produce Canal Intelligence demonstrator v0.1.
4. Create a customer discovery pack and interview at least 10 potential buyers/partners.
5. Identify and rank 20 real commercial canal opportunities.
6. Select one preventative-maintenance failure mode for a low-cost test.
7. Produce a go / pivot / kill decision based on evidence.

## What we are not committing to yet

- replacing Canal & River Trust or any navigation authority;
- operating a freight fleet;
- buying canal infrastructure;
- creating a cryptocurrency or meme coin;
- issuing investment products;
- claiming environmental credits;
- establishing a particular legal structure;
- raising substantial external capital.

Those decisions come later, if the evidence justifies them.

## Repository standard
