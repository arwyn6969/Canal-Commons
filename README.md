# Canal Commons

## Saving Britain's Canals by Making Them Matter

**Canal Commons is an open, evidence-led project exploring how Britain's inland waterways can be protected, renewed and made economically resilient — by the people, for the people.**

We are deliberately testing ideas rather than assuming we already know the answer.

Our current working hypothesis is that the biggest opportunity may be **commercial project origination and aggregation**: finding underdeveloped canal-related opportunities, connecting the right owners, operators, funders and communities, and creating viable projects that generate both financial and public value.

> **We are not building another dashboard for the Canal & River Trust. We are investigating what an independent organisation can do that the existing system cannot easily do itself.**

---

## 🌊 Why this exists

Britain's canals are infrastructure, heritage, transport corridors, public spaces, ecological assets and potential economic infrastructure.

But maintaining a huge network requires more than public funding alone. Climate pressure, ageing infrastructure, operating costs and fragmented opportunities create a difficult long-term problem.

Canal Commons exists to investigate a simple question:

**Can we create a self-sustaining organisation that helps turn canal problems and opportunities into real projects — and gets paid for doing so?**

If the answer is no, we will say so and change direction.

---

## 🎯 What we are doing now

We have deliberately moved through several hypotheses:

- canal monitoring / infrastructure intelligence
- predictive maintenance
- freight
- energy and heat
- environmental finance
- community ownership
- tokenisation
- commercial development
- project finance

Our research has ruled out **building generic canal AI/monitoring as the primary business**. The Canal & River Trust already has substantial asset-management, monitoring and commercial capability.

The current hypothesis under test is therefore:

### **Canal Commons as an independent commercial project aggregator / developer**

Potential roles include:

**Discover → Package → Match → Develop → Transact → Verify → Reinvest**

Possible revenue models include development fees, origination fees, transaction fees, project-management fees, revenue shares and — only where justified — equity or other investment structures.

**No token or cryptocurrency is required for the core model.** Decentralisation/tokenisation remains an optional mechanism to investigate only if it solves a real problem better than conventional infrastructure finance.

## 🔬 Current research

### Active commercial hypotheses

1. **Canalside development / infrastructure interface**
2. **Freight aggregation**
3. **Project-finance packaging**
4. **Utility / energy origination**

Every opportunity must pass the same test:

> **Real project → real counterparty → quantified economics → defensible Canal Commons role → legitimate way to get paid → measurable canal benefit.**

### Current pilot work

The Rochdale Canal intelligence experiment is being retained as a **capability experiment**, not assumed to be the business. It tests whether independent cross-data analysis can create useful incremental information.

### Commercial gate

We are now looking for **real transactions**, not more generic strategy documents.

**No payer = stop.**

**No defensible role = stop.**

**No measurable benefit = stop.**

**No credible route to a transaction within 6–12 months = deprioritise.**

## 🤝 How you can contribute

You do **not** need to be a canal expert or software developer.

Useful contributions include:

- finding a real canal-related commercial opportunity
- identifying a freight flow that could move by water
- finding a funding source or investment programme
- identifying a developer, operator, engineer or landowner with a relevant problem
- challenging an assumption in our research
- providing local canal knowledge
- checking data provenance or licensing

## 📐 Our principles

### 1. Evidence over enthusiasm
Interesting is not enough. We need evidence.

### 2. Commercial sustainability
We intend to build something that can pay the people doing the work. Volunteerism alone is not the business model.

### 3. Additionality
We should add value rather than duplicate what CRT, government, councils, engineers or existing businesses already do well.

### 4. Public benefit
Commercial success must remain aligned with tangible canal, environmental, community or economic benefit.

### 5. Open reasoning
Our assumptions, failures and decisions should be documented so others can challenge them.

### 6. Small experiments first
No large build, token launch, boat purchase or capital raise without evidence.

### 7. Kill weak ideas
A failed hypothesis is useful. Continuing because we like an idea is not.

## 🗺️ Current status

**Phase:** Commercial Opportunity Validation

**Primary question:** Can Canal Commons identify and help originate a real canal-related transaction for which an independent intermediary would be paid?

**Current target:** Find and validate three real transaction opportunities.

**Success condition:** At least one credible paid mandate or equivalent commercial pathway emerges.

**Failure condition:** The market repeatedly demonstrates that the opportunity is already served, economically unattractive, or impossible for an independent organisation to capture.
