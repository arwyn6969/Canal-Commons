# 🌊 START HERE — Canal Commons

Welcome.

Canal Commons is an open experiment asking a simple question:

> **Can communities, businesses, technology and capital work together to create sustainable value that helps Britain's canals thrive?**

We are not assuming the answer is yes. We are trying to find out.

## What are we doing?

We investigate real canal problems and opportunities, then test practical solutions.

Our current focus is commercial opportunity discovery: can an independent organisation identify projects that are useful to the canal network and make them happen in a financially sustainable way?

## What are we NOT doing?

We are not:

- pretending to be the Canal & River Trust;
- building another generic AI chatbot;
- launching a token just because blockchain is interesting;
- assuming every canal problem needs a technology solution;
- treating speculative ideas as established facts.

## How can I help?

**Know a canal?** Submit local knowledge or a real problem.

**Have expertise?** Help with engineering, ecology, planning, finance, freight, energy, law, data or community development.

**Have a project?** Tell us about it.

**Have capital or funding knowledge?** Help identify routes to finance.

**Can build software?** Look for `help wanted` and `good first issue` work.

**Think we're wrong?** Challenge us with evidence.
