Blockchain/tokenisation is optional, not foundational.

Only investigate further if it demonstrably improves:

- contribution records
- transparent project funding

- or another concrete business function.

Before any fungible token, investment product or financial promotion: obtain appropriate legal/regulatory advice.

## Decision gates

**G0 Foundation:** credible evidence process → PASS  
**G1 Opportunity:** broad alternatives considered → PASS / refinement ongoing  
**G2 Commercial:** identifiable payer + painful problem → CURRENT  
**G3 MVP:** cheap demonstrator can test value → NEXT  
**G4 Paid Pilot:** customer pays for measurable outcome → REQUIRED BEFORE SCALE  
**G5 Scale:** repeatable profitable economics → REQUIRED BEFORE MAJOR CAPITAL  
**G6 Capital/Ownership:** asset ownership, investment products or decentralised mechanisms → DEFERRED

## Non-negotiable commercial test

A candidate cannot become the core business unless it has a credible path to:

**first £1 → £100k annual revenue → £1m annual revenue → £10m annual revenue**

with sufficient gross margin to pay professional staff and reinvest in the business.

## Kill / pivot rules

Kill or materially pivot a workstream if:

- there is no identifiable payer;
- the customer problem is not painful enough to budget for;
- existing providers already solve it better;
- regulatory requirements make a small experiment impractical;
- data cannot legally be used;
- canal benefit is cosmetic or unmeasurable;
- gross margins cannot support professional operation;
- capital requirements are disproportionate to evidence;
- the proposition depends primarily on speculative token appreciation or donations.
