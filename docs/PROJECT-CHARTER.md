# Project Charter

## Purpose

Investigate and, if justified, build a complementary community-driven system for protecting and renewing Britain's canals.

## Mission

Mobilise people, capital, information, technology and economically productive activity around specific canal needs, while working constructively with existing navigation authorities, charities, councils, businesses and local communities.

## Principles

1. **Evidence before narrative.** Claims should be traceable to reliable sources.
2. **Physical outcomes first.** The project exists to improve real infrastructure, environment and public benefit.
3. **Complement, don't duplicate.** Existing organisations should be partners where they already have the necessary authority or expertise.
4. **Transparency by default.** Assumptions, funding models, decisions and failures should be documented publicly.
5. **No premature token.** Decentralised technology is a tool, not the mission.
6. **Commercial activity must earn its place.** Freight and other businesses must be tested on real economics.
7. **Measure impact.** Money raised is not enough; we must measure what changed physically or socially.
8. **Community participation matters.** Local knowledge and volunteer contribution are core assets.
9. **Avoid exaggerated crisis claims.** The network faces real funding, ageing-infrastructure and resilience challenges, but the project will not manufacture a crisis for fundraising.
10. **Legal compliance is non-negotiable.** Any financial, token, fundraising or investment mechanism requires appropriate professional advice before launch.

## Scope

The initial scope covers England and Wales, with UK-wide comparisons and learning where relevant. Scotland and Northern Ireland have distinct waterway governance arrangements and may be incorporated as separate workstreams.

## Success condition

The project succeeds if it can demonstrate that a transparent, community-driven network can generate additional resources and/or capability that produce measurable canal benefits at a sustainable cost.

## Current non-decisions

No decision has yet been made about:

- legal entity;
- final brand;
- ownership of physical infrastructure;
- freight operations;
- token issuance;
- blockchain network;
- governance structure;
- fundraising model.

These are outputs of the feasibility process, not starting assumptions.
