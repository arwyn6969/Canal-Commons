# Start Here

Welcome to **Canal Commons**.

This page is for someone arriving at the project for the first time and wondering: **What is this, what are you doing, and how can I help?**

## The short version

Britain's canals are valuable infrastructure, but keeping them resilient over the long term requires money, ideas, people and practical delivery.

Canal Commons is investigating whether an **independent, commercially sustainable organisation** can help create and deliver projects that benefit the waterways and the people who use them.

We do not yet claim to know the final business model.

That is deliberate.

## What we currently believe

Our research has tested several directions. We currently think generic canal-monitoring software is unlikely to be sufficiently differentiated because the Canal & River Trust already has substantial technical and commercial capability.

We are therefore testing a more ambitious proposition:

> **Canal Commons could act as an independent project discovery, aggregation and commercialisation layer for opportunities that are valuable to canals but difficult to originate or finance through existing structures.**

That could eventually include development, freight, energy, environmental projects, infrastructure, funding and community participation.

But it must earn its place through evidence.

## What we are NOT doing

We are not currently:

- launching a cryptocurrency
- buying a fleet of boats
- building a giant software platform
- claiming to replace the Canal & River Trust
- assuming every canal problem is a business opportunity
- raising capital before we have a validated model

Those may be considered later if evidence supports them.

## The current mission

### Find one real transaction.

We want to identify a real project with:

- a real problem or opportunity
- a real owner/counterparty
- a quantifiable economic value
- a clear reason for an independent intermediary to exist
- a legitimate way for Canal Commons to be paid
- a measurable benefit to the canal, community or environment

Then we test whether we can actually help make it happen.

## How to help

You can contribute without being a programmer.

The most valuable things you can bring are:

**Local knowledge** — you know a canal, site, operator or problem.

**Commercial knowledge** — you know a company that needs something moved, built, funded or solved.

**Technical knowledge** — you understand engineering, water, ecology, planning, logistics, energy or data.

**Funding knowledge** — you know where capital or grants could come from.

**Healthy scepticism** — you can show us why an idea will not work.

Please don't feel that you need to agree with us.

The project is stronger if people challenge assumptions early.

**Bring us evidence, bring us opportunities, or bring us reasons we are wrong.**

That is exactly what Canal Commons needs.
