# Canal Commons Licensing Policy

## Purpose

Canal Commons is intended to be an open, reusable public project while protecting legitimate third-party rights and confidential commercial information.

## 1. Software

Original software created for Canal Commons is intended to use the **Apache License 2.0** by default. Individual projects may use another OSI-approved licence when there is a documented reason.

## 2. Documentation and original research

Original documentation, research prose and educational material is intended to use **CC BY 4.0** by default, unless marked otherwise.

## 3. Data

Every dataset or derived dataset should record:

- source;
- date accessed;
- original licence;
- permitted uses;
- transformations performed;
- attribution requirements.

A third-party dataset remains subject to its original licence. Public availability does not automatically mean unrestricted reuse.

## 4. Images, maps and other media

Only material with appropriate rights should be committed. Where a third party owns the material, record the source and licence/permission.

## 5. Commercial information

Confidential negotiations, customer information, unpublished deal terms, proprietary partner information and similar material should not be placed in the public repository.

## 6. Contributions

By submitting a contribution, contributors should clearly identify material they did not create and any licence restrictions that apply. Contributors should not submit material they do not have permission to share.

## 7. Future commercial entity

If Canal Commons establishes a separate commercial entity, its proprietary work and contractual materials may be maintained outside the public repository. The existence of a commercial entity does not change the open licence of material already released under an irrevocable open licence.

## 8. Review

This policy should be reviewed before any major dataset release, tokenisation initiative, commercial IP programme or formation of a legal entity.
