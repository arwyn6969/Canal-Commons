# MVP-01 — Canal Intelligence Demonstrator

**Status:** Ready for execution  
**Gate:** G3 MVP  
**Pilot geography:** Rochdale Canal corridor, provisional  
**Budget principle:** minimum viable evidence, not a production platform

## Objective

Determine whether Canal Commons can turn fragmented public/open geospatial and environmental information into a decision product that a professional customer would consider materially useful and potentially worth paying for.

This is **not** an engineering safety assessment and must never be represented as one.

## Customer problem to test

Potential institutional users may have information scattered across asset systems, GIS, weather, planning, terrain, geology, flood-risk and incident sources. The hypothesis is that an independent cross-source screening layer could improve prioritisation of:

- inspections;
- investigations;
- maintenance planning;
- resilience projects;
- development/commercial opportunities.

## Pilot output

Produce a professional decision pack for a defined canal corridor containing:

1. asset and infrastructure map;
2. hazard/context layers;
3. historical incident layer where legally usable;
4. change/remote-sensing indicators where technically defensible;
5. prioritised screening list;
6. evidence and confidence for every flagged location;
7. suggested next investigation, not an engineering prescription;
8. estimated data-production cost;
9. limitations and false-positive/false-negative risks.

## Initial data families

- Canal & River Trust open datasets and published information;
- Ordnance Survey/open geospatial sources where licence permits;
- Environment Agency flood/environment datasets where licence permits;
- UK meteorological/rainfall datasets where licence permits;
- geological/terrain datasets;
- public planning/development information;
- satellite/remote-sensing sources where access and terms permit;
- publicly documented incidents and closures.

Every dataset must have a provenance/licensing record before commercial reuse.

## Risk-screening concept

For each location, calculate a transparent screening score from:

**Hazard × consequence × change signal × evidence gap**

The score is for prioritisation only. It must not be described as probability of failure, structural safety or an engineering condition rating unless validated by an appropriately qualified professional.

## Success criteria

### Technical
- reproducible workflow;
- source provenance for every material input;
- no critical dependence on an unavailable/private dataset;
- clear confidence rating;
- output understandable without technical training.

### Customer
- at least 10 discovery conversations across likely customer types;
- at least 3 repeated statements of a materially similar unmet need;
- at least 1 customer willing to test the demonstrator against a real decision;
- ideally at least 1 paid pilot or equivalent commercial commitment.

### Economic
Track:
- data acquisition cost;
- analyst/engineering time;
- tooling cost;
- cost per corridor/km/asset;
- potential annual subscription value;
- gross-margin potential.

## Customer discovery targets

Prioritise conversations with:

1. engineering/infrastructure consultancies;
2. local authorities;
3. infrastructure owners/operators;
4. insurers/risk specialists;
5. developers/energy companies;
6. Canal & River Trust or other navigation authorities.

Ask about actual decisions, current workflow, data gaps, cost of delay/failure and existing suppliers. Do not lead with the Canal Commons solution.

## Kill criteria

Stop or pivot this MVP if:

- the best available information is already supplied more effectively by an incumbent;
- users cannot identify a decision it would change;
- no plausible payer or budget owner emerges;
- commercial data licensing makes the model uneconomic;
- the signal quality is too weak to beat current practice;
- delivery cost makes recurring margins unattractive.
