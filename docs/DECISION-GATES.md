# Canal Commons — Decision Gates

**Status:** Active project-control document  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Date:** 2026-08-28

## Purpose

These gates prevent Canal Commons from drifting from an evidence-led venture into an attractive but unfunded idea, technology project, charity, token scheme or asset-heavy operator.

The rule is simple:

> **Do not spend heavily, scale nationally, raise substantial capital or commit to a legal/financial architecture until the evidence required by the relevant gate exists.**

## G0 — Foundation

**Question:** Is the project documented, evidence-led and professionally controlled?

### Pass criteria
- clear mission and scope;
- research/source standard;
- visible assumptions and hypotheses;
- GitHub audit trail;
- no premature commitment to legal structure or token.

**Status: PASS**

## G1 — Opportunity

**Question:** Have we genuinely considered alternatives rather than anchoring on the first idea?

### Pass criteria
- broad opportunity longlist;
- commercial and non-commercial alternatives compared;
- outliers considered;
- conventional alternatives used as controls;
- initial scoring includes uncertainty/confidence.

**Status: PASS, with ongoing refinement**

## G2 — Commercial Problem

**Question:** Is there a painful problem with a real payer and a credible budget?

### Required evidence
- minimum 10 customer/partner discovery conversations;
- at least 3 repeated unmet-need signals;
- identifiable buyer and economic decision-maker;
- clear current alternative/substitute;
- plausible price range;
- evidence that the problem is worth paying to solve.

**Status: IN PROGRESS**

## G3 — MVP

**Question:** Can we demonstrate the proposed value cheaply and credibly?

### Required evidence
- defined pilot geography/use case;
- legally usable data;
- reproducible methodology;
- clear output a customer can use;
- baseline against current practice;
- explicit cost ceiling;
- measurable success/failure criteria.

**Status: NEXT**

## G4 — Paid Pilot

**Question:** Will someone actually pay for the demonstrated outcome?

### Pass criteria
At least one credible customer signs a paid pilot, paid discovery engagement, purchase order or equivalent commercial commitment.

Preferred evidence:
- cash payment;
- signed scope;
- defined success metric;
- customer reference rights where possible.

A letter of interest without commercial commitment is useful but does **not** equal a full pass.

**Status: NOT STARTED**

## G5 — Repeatable Business

**Question:** Is there a repeatable and profitable business rather than a successful one-off project?

### Required evidence
- repeatable customer acquisition;
- repeatable delivery;
- positive gross margin;
- professional founder/staff compensation supported by revenue;
- credible recurring or repeat transaction revenue;
- customer retention/renewal evidence;
- credible path toward £1m annual revenue;
- explicit path toward £10m if scale is part of the thesis.

**Status: NOT STARTED**

## G6 — Capital / Ownership / Decentralisation

**Question:** Should Canal Commons raise significant capital, own physical assets, issue financial products or introduce decentralised technology?

### Required evidence
- G5 passed or a compelling asset-specific exception;
- quantified risk/return case;
- legal/regulatory review;
- governance and conflicts framework;
- capital-use plan;
- downside case;
- clear reason the proposed structure is superior to simpler alternatives.

### Token rule
A token is permitted only where its utility is demonstrably superior to ordinary contracts, databases, memberships, points, shares or other conventional mechanisms. Speculative appreciation is not a business case.

**Status: DEFERRED**

## Current sequence

**G0 PASS → G1 PASS → G2 NOW → G3 NEXT → G4 REQUIRED → G5 REQUIRED → G6 OPTIONAL**

## Spend discipline

Until G4:

- prefer research, interviews and small demonstrators;
- avoid large software builds;
- avoid buying boats or infrastructure;
- avoid significant inventory or staffing commitments;
- avoid financial-product/token launches;
- use fixed experimental budgets and kill criteria.

After G4 but before G5:

- scale only the parts with demonstrated customer value;
- measure gross margin and delivery cost;
- build sales pipeline before expanding infrastructure.

## Decision record

Every gate decision should record:

1. date;
2. evidence considered;
3. assumptions tested;
4. result;
5. confidence;
6. decision: **GO / PIVOT / KILL / HOLD**;
7. next measurable action;
8. person responsible.

This document is a project-control mechanism, not a promise that any particular business model will succeed.
