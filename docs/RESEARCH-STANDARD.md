# Research & Evidence Standard

Canal Commons is an evidence-first project. Research should be reproducible and auditable.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. UK Government, Parliament, regulators and statutory bodies
2. Canal & River Trust and other navigation authorities
3. Local authorities and official public bodies
4. Audited accounts and formal filings
5. Academic and engineering research
6. Established industry bodies
7. Reputable journalism
8. Community reports and social media, clearly labelled as anecdotal

## Every important claim should record

- claim;
- source;
- publication/update date;
- access date;
- relevant page/section where practical;
- whether the figure is reported, calculated or estimated;
- confidence level.

## Financial modelling

Models must distinguish:

- observed figures;
- assumptions;
- derived calculations;
- scenarios;
- forecasts.

Never present a scenario as an established fact.

## Contradictory evidence

Where credible sources disagree, record the disagreement rather than selecting the convenient number.

## Research log

Each major research workstream should eventually have a dated research note under `research/` and, where practical, a machine-readable dataset or calculation under `data/` or `models/`.
