# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Canal Commons is intended to be a welcoming, evidence-led community for people with different backgrounds, skills and viewpoints.

We expect participants to:

- treat others with respect;
- debate ideas rather than attack people;
- welcome good-faith disagreement;
- distinguish evidence from opinion;
- credit other people's work;
- respect confidentiality and privacy;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and personal abuse.

## Good-faith challenge is welcome

Canal Commons is explicitly designed to challenge its own assumptions. Saying that an idea is wrong, commercially unviable or poorly evidenced is welcome when done constructively.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of confidential information, impersonation, spam and repeated disruptive behaviour.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, close, lock or remove contributions that breach these standards. Serious or repeated breaches may result in loss of participation privileges.

If you believe there is a problem, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub or the project's published support route.
