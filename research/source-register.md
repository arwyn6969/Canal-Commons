## Research rule

Primary sources should be preferred for financial, legal, engineering and governance claims. Secondary sources may be used for context, but material claims should be traceable to a primary source wherever practical.

## Important distinction

A source can establish what an organisation **says**. It does not automatically establish that the statement is independently verified. Research notes must distinguish:

- reported fact
- independently verified fact
- calculation derived from reported figures
- project assumption
- hypothesis requiring testing.
