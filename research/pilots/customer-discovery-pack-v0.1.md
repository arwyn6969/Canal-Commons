# Canal Commons Customer Discovery Pack v0.1

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Purpose:** Validate whether Canal Commons can solve a paid problem before building software or raising capital.

## Target groups

1. Canal/navigation asset managers
2. Civil and geotechnical engineering firms
3. Local authorities / combined authorities
4. Water/environmental consultancies
5. Developers and regeneration partners
6. Energy/heat-network developers
7. Infrastructure insurers / brokers
8. Infrastructure investors / lenders
9. Freight/logistics operators
10. Specialist monitoring/geospatial companies

## Interview rule

Do not pitch the Canal Intelligence product first. Diagnose the existing workflow and budget.

## Core questions

### Problem
- What canal/waterway infrastructure decisions are hardest to make with the information currently available?
- Where do you experience avoidable uncertainty?
- Which failures are most expensive or disruptive when discovered late?
- What information do you wish you had before inspection, procurement, development or investment?

### Current solution
- What systems/data do you use today?
- Who owns the decision?
- What does the current process cost in staff time, consultants, surveys or delay?
- What information is duplicated or difficult to reconcile?

### Economics
- What budget pays for this today?
- What would a better answer be worth?
- Is the value mainly cost reduction, risk reduction, revenue generation, compliance, speed or evidence for investment?
- What would prevent procurement?

### Product test
Only after diagnosis:
- If you received a corridor-level map ranking where investigation or investment deserves attention, what decision would it change?
- What evidence would you require before trusting it?
- Would you commission a one-off pilot? At what approximate budget band?
- Would a recurring data/analytics service be more useful?

### Commercial validation
A positive signal requires one of:
- request for a proposal;
- introduction to the budget owner;
- agreement to test against a live decision;
- paid discovery/pilot discussion.

A compliment, generic enthusiasm or statement that the idea is 'interesting' does not count.

## Proposed offer hypotheses

### Offer A — Corridor Risk/Opportunity Brief
Fixed-scope professional report and map for a defined corridor.

### Offer B — Decision Intelligence Pilot
Short paid engagement combining external data, asset context and prioritisation for a defined decision.

### Offer C — Recurring Intelligence Service
Quarterly/monthly monitoring and prioritisation for organisations with repeated infrastructure decisions.

### Offer D — Project Origination
Identify, quantify and package a commercially viable canal-related project and earn an agreed origination/development fee only where value is created.

## Validation target

- 10–15 substantive conversations.
- At least 3 independent reports of the same material unmet need.
- At least 2 potential customers willing to review a demonstrator against a live decision.
- At least 1 credible paid-pilot opportunity.

## Red flags

- No budget owner.
- Existing provider already solves the problem materially better.
- Data licensing prevents the product.
- Buyer wants a free bespoke consultancy service rather than a scalable product.
- Claimed canal benefit cannot be measured.

## Recording standard

Each conversation should be anonymised in repository notes unless the participant explicitly authorises attribution. Record:
- organisation type;
- role type;
- problem;
- current workaround;
- economic consequence;
- data gap;
- buying process;
- willingness to test/pay;
- objections;
- follow-up.
