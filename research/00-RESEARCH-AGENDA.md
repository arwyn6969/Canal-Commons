# Research Agenda — v0.1

## A. Network and governance

- What waterways are covered by each navigation authority?
- What assets and responsibilities sit with CRT, Environment Agency, local authorities and others?
- What changed in 2012 and what is the current governance model?

## B. Funding

- Government grant history and 2027–2037 settlement
- CRT income and expenditure
- Investment/endowment structure
- Maintenance and capital expenditure
- Inflation-adjusted funding gap
- Funding requirements by asset class

## C. Infrastructure condition

- Locks
- Embankments
- Reservoirs
- Culverts
- Bridges
- Tunnels
- Towpaths
- Water-control structures

## D. Failure and resilience

Build case studies for recent breaches, closures and major engineering interventions. For each: cause, warning signs, response, cost, duration, disruption and lessons.

## E. Climate

Assess flooding, drought, extreme rainfall, heat, subsidence, vegetation and other climate-related pressures.

## F. Economics

- Tourism
- Boating
- Towpath recreation
- Property/regeneration
- Commercial moorings
- Freight
- Waste and aggregates
- Renewable energy
- Water management

## G. Community

Map existing volunteer groups, canal societies, adoption schemes, schools, charities and local campaigns. Identify gaps rather than duplicating successful programmes.

## H. Freight feasibility

Identify realistic corridors, cargo types, origin/destination pairs, loading infrastructure, vessel requirements, costs, customers and potential contribution to infrastructure maintenance.

## I. Digital infrastructure

Assess open data, GIS, citizen reporting, sensors, AI-assisted triage and public project dashboards.

## J. Legal and financial structure

Compare charity, CIC, company, cooperative and hybrid structures. Separately assess fundraising, investment, financial promotion and tokenisation rules before any launch.
